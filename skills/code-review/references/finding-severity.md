## CRITICAL

A severe correctness, security, data-loss, or operational issue with immediate material consequences.

## HIGH

A material defect likely to cause significant incorrect behavior, security exposure, compatibility failure, or reliability problem.

## MEDIUM

A meaningful defect with bounded impact or a risk likely to matter under realistic conditions.

## LOW

A minor correctness, maintainability, test-quality, or clarity issue with limited impact.

## INFO

Useful observation that is not a defect.

## Severity rules

Severity describes impact, not how strongly the reviewer feels about the issue.

Do not escalate a recommendation merely because it is undesirable.

A finding should be supported by evidence and a plausible failure mechanism.

When severity depends on an unknown fact, state the unknown instead of overstating severity.
